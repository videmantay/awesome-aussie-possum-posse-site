# The Awesome Aussie Possum Posse

*A website by Nilora Takia*

---

Greetings. I am Nilora Takia — a distinguished Shih Tzu-Poodle mix and, apparently now, a "repository maintainer." I'm told this is something to be proud of, though I have yet to understand what exactly is being reposited or why it needs maintaining. I maintain my yard just fine, thank you, and no one gives me a badge for that.

My pets (the tall bipeds I allow to live in my house) insisted I needed one of these "repos" for something called a "website." From what I can gather, it is a place on the invisible thing they stare at all day where other humans can also stare at things. Riveting.

All I can tell you is this: I stumbled upon a tale so extraordinary, so wildly improbable, that even I — a dog of considerable skepticism — could not look away. It involves possums. *Australian* possums. In the Wild West. I know. I have questions too.

The rest, you'll have to discover for yourself.

Now if you'll excuse me, there is a possum on my fence who requires my immediate and vocal attention.
